### Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|------|----------------|------------|
| **Regulatory scrutiny** | Nigerian central bank controls crypto‑fiat exchanges; P2P desks may be frozen. | Use regulated partners (Yativo, Cleva) and keep KYC docs handy. |
| **Conversion slippage & hidden fees** | Exchange brokers add spread; P2P often uses black‑market rates. | Always read the *quoted rate* and *posted fee*; compare Cleva vs Yativo vs walllet.com. |
| **Transaction delays** | Multi‑hop routes (exchange → bank) add time & risk of reversal. | Stick to single‑hop off‑ramp solutions (e.g., Yativo’s licensed payout). |
| **Account limits & freeze** | Small balances may trigger 0‑balance alerts; large transfers may be flagged. | Keep a minimum buffer in the wallet; post invoices & receipts for large payouts. |
| **Network congestion** | Celo or Ethereum (bridge) can become slow, raising gas and delay payouts. | Monitor network fees; use lower‑gas times or wait for Celo L1 improvements. |
| **Counterparty risk** | The P2P seller may not deliver the NGN. | Use escrow or a licensed payout partner; avoid unverified desks. |
